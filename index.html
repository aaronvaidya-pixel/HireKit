Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

HireKit™ is built and operated by Aaron Vaidya. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password (stored locally in your browser)
  • Usage data: which profiles you have opened (stored locally, not on our servers)
  • JD kit requests: job title, keywords, and notes you submit via the Request a Kit form
  • Feedback: ratings and comments you submit voluntarily

How We Store Your Data

HireKit currently stores all account data in your browser's localStorage. This means your data stays on your device and is never sent to our servers. If you clear your browser data, your account will be removed.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your data to any third party
  • We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies
  • We do not store passwords in plain text (they are stored only in your local browser)
  • We do not send you marketing emails without your consent

JD Kit Requests

When you submit a Request a Kit form, your job title, name, email, keywords, and notes are sent to Aaron Vaidya at contact@aaronvaidya.com for the purpose of building a custom screening kit. This data is not shared with anyone else.

Contact

For any privacy questions, email contact@aaronvaidya.com
